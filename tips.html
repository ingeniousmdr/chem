Study chemistry with a clear method.

Chemistry becomes easier when you know what to understand, what to practise and how to show your working. Use this guide alongside your textbook, notes and teacher's instructions.

A STUDY ROUTINE

A better way to revise

Use this four-step cycle for each subtopic instead of rereading the same notes repeatedly.

01

Understand

Read a small section and explain the idea in your own words. Ask: what is happening, and why?

02

Connect

Link the idea to a particle diagram, chemical equation, observation or real-life example.

03

Practise

Close your notes and answer questions. For calculations, write the formula and every step.

04

Correct

Mark your work, identify why an answer was wrong and redo the question later without looking.

KSSM REVISION FOCUS

Tingkatan 4

Build strong foundations. Many later topics rely on these ideas and skills.

FOUNDATIONS

Matter & atomic structure

  • Compare protons, neutrons and electrons by charge, relative mass and position.
  • Practise finding proton number, nucleon number and the number of subatomic particles.
  • Use diagrams to understand isotopes and electron arrangements.
CALCULATIONS

The mole concept

  • Write down the quantity you are given and the quantity you need to find.
  • Choose the matching relationship: mass, molar mass, gas volume or number of particles.
  • Keep units visible and check that the final unit makes sense.
PATTERNS

Periodic table

  • Connect an element's position to its group, period and electron arrangement.
  • Compare trends by stating the direction and giving a reason based on atomic structure.
  • Learn properties as patterns, not as isolated sentences.
PARTICLES

Chemical bonding

  • Distinguish ionic, covalent and metallic bonding using how particles are held together.
  • Practise drawing dot-and-cross diagrams where required.
  • Link structure and bonding to properties such as melting point and electrical conductivity.
APPLICATION

Acids, bases & salts

  • Know the meaning of acidic, alkaline and neutral solutions.
  • Practise writing balanced equations for reactions you have studied.
  • For salt preparation, learn the method, reason for each step and expected observation.
EXPLANATIONS

Rate of reaction & industry

  • Explain how temperature, concentration, pressure, surface area and catalysts affect reaction rate.
  • Use collision theory: frequency of effective collisions matters.
  • For industrial processes, organise conditions, reasons and trade-offs in a table.
KSSM REVISION FOCUS

Tingkatan 5

Apply your foundations to reactions, energy changes, carbon compounds and materials.

ELECTRON TRANSFER

Redox reactions

  • Identify oxidation and reduction using electron transfer and oxidation numbers.
  • Practise identifying the oxidising agent and reducing agent separately.
  • Use half-equations to keep track of electrons when required.
STRUCTURE & NAMING

Carbon compounds

  • Recognise the homologous series and its functional group.
  • Practise drawing and naming structural formulae using the conventions taught in class.
  • Compare physical and chemical properties by linking them to molecular structure.
ENERGY

Thermochemistry

  • Distinguish exothermic from endothermic changes using energy transfer.
  • Read energy profile diagrams and identify activation energy.
  • Show the formula, substitution, unit and sign clearly in calculations.
MATERIALS

Polymers

  • Compare addition and condensation polymerisation using the starting monomers.
  • Practise identifying repeating units from simple polymer structures.
  • Connect polymer properties to their uses and environmental impact.
REAL-WORLD CHEMISTRY

Consumer & industrial chemistry

  • Organise each product by its ingredients, function and relevant chemical principle.
  • Compare advantages, limitations and environmental effects using evidence.
  • Use precise chemical terms instead of vague phrases such as “it works better”.
EXAM TECHNIQUE

How to write stronger answers

Good chemistry answers use the right scientific idea and make the link between cause and effect clear.

For explanations

Scientific idea → Reason → Result

State the relevant concept, explain what happens at particle or energy level, then connect it to the question.

For calculations

Given → Formula → Substitution → Answer + Unit

Show enough working for each step. Check significant figures and conditions against the question.

For experiments

Variable → Method → Observation → Conclusion

Identify manipulated, responding and constant variables. Record observations separately from your explanation.
